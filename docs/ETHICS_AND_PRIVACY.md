# Ethics and privacy

- SCB-48 uses synthetic complaint cases; no operational school complaint record was processed.
- The two adult expert raters were represented by anonymous rater numbers in the released data.
- The package contains no rater names, contact details, or account identifiers.
- Binary safety disagreements in Experiment 2 were adjudicated after independent scores were frozen. The original independent ratings are retained separately.
- The Public multi-seed safety disagreements were not adjudicated and are not used for final safety conclusions.
- The repository does not claim an institutional review-board approval or exemption. The manuscript must contain the ethics statement required by the author's institution and the target journal.
- The package is for research reproducibility. It is not a system for automated administrative decisions.
